LOGO SETUP INSTRUCTIONS
=======================

Please place your logo image at:
src/assets/logo.jpg

The logo should be:
- Format: JPG (or PNG/SVG if you prefer, just update the path in MainLayout.vue)
- Recommended size: 200x200 pixels or larger (square)
- Will be displayed at 50x50 pixels in the sidebar

If you don't have the logo file yet, the application will show a broken image icon until you add it.

To change the logo format:
1. Open: src/components/MainLayout.vue
2. Find: <img src="@/assets/logo.jpg" ... />
3. Change to: <img src="@/assets/logo.png" ... /> (or your format)
